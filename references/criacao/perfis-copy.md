# Copy Profiles

A copy profile is a reusable writing style applied to the whole blueprint. It changes tone, proof structure, headline patterns, and CTA language — never the model or the layout.

## Canonical profile format

Create new profiles with these five sections:

1. **Tone** — voice, register, and emotional temperature.
2. **Proof structure** — how claims are supported (numbers, testimonials, authority, urgency).
3. **Headline patterns** — reusable templates with the promise first.
4. **CTA language** — verbs and framing per stage.
5. **Anti-patterns** — what this profile must never do.

## Profile: Giveaway

- **Tone:** generous, fast, low-commitment. The reader wins something with minimal effort.
- **Proof structure:** the prize itself is the proof — show it concretely (what, how many, deadline). Social proof optional; scarcity of the reward is the lever.
- **Headline patterns:** "Ganhe [recompensa] — [limite de tempo]" · "Sorteio: [recompensa] para [público]" · "[Recompensa] grátis: participe até [data]".
- **CTA language:** "Quero participar" · "Garantir meu número" · "Participar agora".
- **Anti-patterns:** never invent winners or previous results; never bury the participation deadline; never require payment for a "giveaway".

## Profile: Empiricus

- **Tone:** analyst voice — authoritative, editorial, urgent without hysteria. Data and market context lead; the offer follows the analysis.
- **Proof structure:** numbers with sources, historical context, named risks. Authority first, offer second, deadline last.
- **Headline patterns:** "O que [fato de mercado] significa para [público]" · "[Número] que [consequência] — veja o que fazer" · "Relatório: [tema] em [período]".
- **CTA language:** "Acessar o relatório completo" · "Quero a análise" · "Liberar minha leitura".
- **Anti-patterns:** never fabricate performance claims, past returns, or analyst credentials; never promise results; every market claim carries its source or is dropped.

## Choosing a profile

The profile is chosen at creation (a quick decision, like the five in `geracao.md`) and can be changed later by a Mini-Lovable edit. A profile never overrides the anti-fabrication contract: no invented numbers, testimonials, or credentials in any profile.
