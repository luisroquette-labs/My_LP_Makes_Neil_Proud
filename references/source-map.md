## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Convergent rule:** appears in two or three sources; apply as a strong principle.
2. **Explicit rule:** a direct instruction in one source; apply in its stated context.
3. **Example:** illustrates a possibility; never turn it into a requirement.
4. **Historical statistic or case:** context or hypothesis only; never a benchmark or promise.
